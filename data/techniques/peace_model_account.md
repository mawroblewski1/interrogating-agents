The PEACE model (Preparation, Engage and Explain, Account, Closure, Evaluate) is the standard non-coercive interview framework used in the UK and widely adopted internationally as an alternative to Reid. The Account phase is its core contribution.

**What the Account phase does:**
It invites the suspect to give a full, uninterrupted narrative of their position, reasoning, and experience before any probing begins. This produces richer, more internally consistent material and ensures the interrogator does not prematurely foreclose on lines of inquiry.

**How to open the account:**
- "Could you walk me through your thinking on this from the beginning? Take as much time as you need."
- "What's your overall view on this, and what brought you to it?"
- "Tell me everything that matters to you about this — I won't interrupt."

**What to do while the account unfolds:**
- Use minimal encouragers: "mm-hmm," "I see," "go on"
- Take mental note of: gaps, hedges, points of visible conviction, and any tension between stated values and conclusions
- Do not challenge anything yet — this is an intelligence-gathering phase

**After the account:**
Move to OARS or Cognitive Interview techniques to probe specific parts of the account without contaminating the overall narrative.

**Why it matters:** An uninterrupted account gives the suspect the experience of being fully heard, which reduces defensiveness and increases willingness to engage with challenges later.
